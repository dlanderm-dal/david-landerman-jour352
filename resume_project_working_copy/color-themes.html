Accent colour options

Each card is a miniature of the five places the accent actually appears on your site: the section band, the bar beside your name, the active nav marker, the open tile's top edge, and the badge / button / date text. The chips under each one are real contrast measurements. The orange you have now fails two of them, including the section headers: white on #FF6B35 is 2.8:1, under even the relaxed 3:1 bar for large text. That is why the badge and the dates had to be hand-darkened three separate times.

Reading the chips. Header text is the white "Work Experience" on the band (large, so 3:1 is enough). Button label is white on the same colour at button size, which needs 4.5:1. Date text is the colour used as small text on white, like Expected Graduation: May 2027. Badge text is the honours badge sitting on a 13% wash of itself. Where a colour is too light for one of the text jobs, the snippet hands you a darkened companion shade for that job rather than failing — that is what the +1 shade label means. One shade means the flat colour does every job by itself.
